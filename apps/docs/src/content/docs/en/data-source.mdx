---
title: Data Sources and Limitations
description: List of all data in the Politigraph database
---

The complete list of data in the Politigraph database consists of

| Data                                                                                                    | Source                                                                                                                                                              | Update Frequency                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Limitations                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Votes on bills in the 1st and 3rd readings by Representatives and the National Assembly (joint sitting) | Meeting Report and Minutes Database System, Secretariat of the House of Representatives (<span style="word-break: break-all">https://msbis.parliament.go.th</span>) | **Weekly**<ul><li>The automation system pulls data from the website every Monday morning and converts it into a machine-readable file</li><li>The team cross-checks the data against the source documents the same evening</li></ul>                                   | <ul><li>Our data matches what is published on the source website. If a vote happens but the website hasn't published it within the week we collect data, it won't appear in our system either</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Votes on bills in the 1st and 3rd readings by Senators                                                  | <ul><li>The Senate Secretariat's notice board, alongside requesting data under the Official Information Act B.E. 2540 (1997)</li></ul>                              | **Monthly**<ul><li>We coordinate with the Senate Secretariat in the last week of the month and convert the data into a machine-readable file</li><li>The team cross-checks the data against the source documents on the Friday of the last week of the month</li></ul> | <ul><li>The volume and completeness of the data depends on the Secretariat's discretion</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Introduction and consideration of bills                                                                 | **Legislative Information System**, Secretariat of the House of Representatives (<span style="word-break: break-all">https://lis.parliament.go.th</span>)           | **Weekly**<ul><li>The automation system pulls data from the website every Monday morning and converts it into a machine-readable file</li><li>The team cross-checks the data against the source documents the same evening</li></ul>                                   | <ul><li>Our data matches what is published on the source website. If a bill is introduced or considered but the website hasn't published it within the week we collect data, it won't appear in our system either</li><li>Linking vote data to a bill's consideration status requires data from both source websites. If either website hasn't published the data needed to link them, the two parts cannot yet be linked</li></ul> |
| Votes to elect the Prime Minister                                                                       | News media                                                                                                                                                          | **Ad hoc**<ul><li>When there is a Prime Minister vote agenda</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                 | <ul><li>We started collecting data from the 27th House of Representatives (2026)</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Political office holding (Representatives / Senators / Cabinet)                                         | <ul><li>Royal Gazette announcements (<span style="word-break: break-all">https://ratchakitcha.soc.go.th</span>)</li><li>News media</li></ul>                        | **Ad hoc**<ul><li>After an election result is certified</li><li>When a new Cabinet is formed</li><li>When there is news about someone taking office, changing position, or leaving office</li></ul>                                                                    | <ul><li>Data may not be updated if the change was not reported as news, or if the team did not come across the relevant news</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Representatives' party affiliation and party switching (only during a parliamentary session)            | <ul><li>Candidacy filing documents from the Election Commission of Thailand (ECT)</li><li>News media</li></ul>                                                      | **Ad hoc**<ul><li>After an election result is certified</li><li>When there is news about a party switch</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                      | <ul><li>Data may not be updated if the change was not reported as news, or if the team did not come across the relevant news</li><li>News media sources do not publish an official or written record of party membership, so the start and end dates of party membership are inferred from the dates and times mentioned in the news</li></ul>                                                                                      |
| Governing coalition and opposition status of parties                                                    | News media                                                                                                                                                          | **Ad hoc**<ul><li>When a new Cabinet is formed</li><li>When there is news about a change in position</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                         | <ul><li>Data may not be updated if the change was not reported as news, or if the team did not come across the relevant news</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Politicians' basic information (age, gender, education, previous occupation)                            | <ul><li>News media</li><li>Politicians' social media</li></ul>                                                                                                      | **Ad hoc**<ul><li>The team fills in this data gradually as resources allow, prioritizing completeness and usefulness to users</li></ul>                                                                                                                                | <ul><li>Updates to this data are prioritized after data needed to monitor and scrutinize the legislature's work. The team aims to fill it in as completely and continuously as possible</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
